Alternate Picking

★★★★☆

Build clean up/down strokes with a metronome.

Goal: consistent timing and tone across all notes.

  • Start at a tempo you can play perfectly for 30 seconds
  • Increase by 2–5 BPM only when clean
  • Keep pick movement small and relaxed

Legato

★★★☆☆

Hammer-ons and pull-offs for smooth, flowing lines.

Goal: even volume without string noise.

  • Keep fingers close to the fretboard (don’t lift high)
  • Use controlled pull-offs (don’t yank)
  • Practice 3 notes per string patterns slowly first

Palm Muting

★★☆☆☆

Control heaviness and rhythm by muting near the bridge.

Goal: consistent mute pressure and tight rhythm.

  • Rest the edge of your palm lightly near the bridge
  • Practice switching between open and muted notes
  • Lock to a metronome and focus on downbeats

Downpicking Endurance

★★★★☆

Build tight rhythm and stamina for metal riffs.

Goal: steady downstrokes without speeding up.

  • Choose one muted riff pattern and loop it for 30–60 seconds
  • Keep wrist relaxed; no elbow punching
  • Increase BPM slowly once it feels controlled

String Skipping

★★★★☆

Improve picking accuracy across non-adjacent strings.

Goal: clean jumps without hitting extra strings.

  • Practice slow alternate picking patterns first
  • Use light muting to control noise
  • Stay relaxed and let accuracy lead speed

Sweep Picking

★★★★★

Arpeggio technique using a continuous pick motion.

Goal: smooth string transitions with minimal noise.

  • Start with 3-string shapes before moving to 5/6 strings
  • Mute unused strings with your fretting hand
  • Work slow and sync both hands before increasing BPM

Weekly Practice Structure

Use one technique per day, then apply it to a song.

  • 5 min: warmup (chromatic + stretching)
  • 10 min: technique focus (one expanded card)
  • 10 min: metronome timing work
  • 10 min: apply technique to a riff or song section
