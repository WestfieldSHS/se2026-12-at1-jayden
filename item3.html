Songs / Influencers

Use songs and creators as learning tools — choose pieces that train specific skills.

How to learn a song

  1. Listen and identify sections (intro/verse/chorus)
  2. Learn one section at a time (slow)
  3. Loop the hardest bar until clean
  4. Practise transitions between sections
  5. Play along with a backing track

Song ideas

  • Easy: timing + chord changes
  • Medium: riffs + alternate picking
  • Hard: solos + speed + endurance

Add your favourite songs and write what each one improves.

Creators to learn from

  • Pick creators who explain clearly
  • Choose people in the genre you like
  • Compare multiple tutorials

Practice focus ideas

  • One riff per day
  • One hard bar per session
  • Record yourself weekly to track progress
