Single Strokes

★★☆☆☆

Build clean RLRL strokes with even timing.

Goal: even volume and steady tempo.

  • Start slow with a metronome (e.g., 60–80 BPM)
  • Keep wrists relaxed, don’t tense your forearms
  • Increase 2–5 BPM only when it sounds clean

Double Strokes

★★★☆☆

Develop bounce control for RRLL patterns.

Goal: consistent doubles without rushing.

  • Let the stick rebound—don’t force the second hit
  • Practice slow first, then build speed gradually
  • Use even spacing between each note

Paradiddles

★★★☆☆

Improve coordination and sticking control.

Goal: clean sticking and controlled accents.

  • Start with RLRR LRLL very slowly
  • Add accents on the first note of each group
  • Move the pattern around the kit once consistent

Hi-Hat Timing & Groove

★★☆☆☆

Lock your groove by controlling subdivisions.

Goal: steady time that feels “tight”.

  • Practice 8th notes, then 16th notes with a metronome
  • Keep volume consistent, avoid random accents
  • Record yourself and listen for rushing/dragging

Kick Drum Control

★★★★☆

Build foot stamina, accuracy, and consistency.

Goal: consistent hits with no fatigue breakdown.

  • Start with slow patterns (quarter notes → eighths)
  • Keep ankle relaxed and avoid “stomping”
  • Increase tempo once you can hold it for 30–60 seconds

Fills & Transitions

★★★★☆

Move around the kit cleanly without losing time.

Goal: fills that land perfectly back on beat 1.

  • Count the fill (e.g., “1-e-&-a”) to stay in time
  • Practice one-bar fills into a basic groove
  • Keep the last note controlled so you don’t rush the return

Weekly Practice Structure

Use one technique per day, then apply it to a groove or song.

  • 5 min: warmup (singles + doubles)
  • 10 min: technique focus (one expanded card)
  • 10 min: timing practice (metronome subdivisions)
  • 10 min: apply it to a beat or song section
