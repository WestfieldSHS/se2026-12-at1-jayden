Scales (Major/Minor)

★★☆☆☆

Build finger control, even tone, and timing.

Goal: even volume and steady rhythm across the scale.

  • Start hands separately, then combine once clean
  • Use a metronome and focus on even spacing
  • Keep wrists relaxed and fingers curved

Arpeggios

★★★☆☆

Improve chord movement and smooth finger transitions.

Goal: smooth thumb-under motion with consistent tone.

  • Practice slowly and keep hand relaxed
  • Avoid “bumping” the thumb under, make it smooth
  • Use a metronome and increase speed gradually

Finger Independence (Hanon Style)

★★★☆☆

Strengthen weaker fingers and improve control.

Goal: clean, even notes without tension.

  • Play lightly — don’t “slam” the keys
  • Keep fingers close to the keys for control
  • Stop if hands get tense; accuracy first

Chord Voicings & Inversions

★★☆☆☆

Make chord changes smoother and more musical.

Goal: smooth chord transitions with minimal movement.

  • Practice I–V–vi–IV in multiple keys
  • Choose inversions that keep fingers close
  • Listen for balance: melody louder than harmony

Sight Reading

★★★★☆

Read music smoothly without stopping.

Goal: keep a steady tempo and recover from mistakes.

  • Count aloud and keep going (don’t restart)
  • Look ahead by 1–2 beats while playing
  • Read easy music daily for 5–10 minutes

Pedal Control

★★★☆☆

Use sustain pedal cleanly without muddy sound.

Goal: clean changes that match chord transitions.

  • Change pedal when the chord changes (lift then press)
  • Practice half pedal for smoother sustain control
  • Record yourself to check if it sounds muddy

Weekly Practice Structure

Use one technique per day, then apply it to a piece.

  • 5 min: warmup (easy scales + finger stretch)
  • 10 min: technique focus (one expanded card)
  • 10 min: metronome timing work
  • 10 min: apply technique to a short piece or section
