Sheet Music

Learn notation and rhythm so you can read simple parts confidently.

Core notation basics

  • Staff + clef (where notes sit)
  • Time signatures (beats per bar)
  • Note values: whole, half, quarter, eighth
  • Rests = silence (count them)

Counting rhythm

4/4: count “1 2 3 4” evenly.

Eighth notes: count “1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &”.

Clap the rhythm before playing.

Sight-reading routine

  • Choose an easy piece and start slow
  • Clap the rhythm first
  • Play once without stopping
  • Repeat and fix one mistake at a time

Common mistakes

  • Ignoring rhythm to find notes
  • Starting too fast
  • Stopping every time there’s an error

Mini challenge

Pick a simple melody and practise 5 minutes daily at a slow tempo.

Goal: smooth rhythm with no stopping.
