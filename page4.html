About MusicTech

Why this site exists and who it is for.

Purpose

MusicTech helps learners choose a topic and follow simple routines so practice becomes consistent and progress is easier to track.

Audience

This website is aimed at beginner, to intermediate music students who want structure, guidance, and motivation.

How to use the site

  • Go to Home and choose a topic card
  • Follow the routines on each topic page
  • Practise a little each day
