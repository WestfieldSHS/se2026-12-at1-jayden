Page 2: Techniques

Short practice plans to build timing, control, and clean technique.

Daily 10-minute warmup

  • 1 min: gentle finger stretches
  • 3 min: chromatic exercise (slow and clean)
  • 3 min: alternate picking on one string
  • 3 min: simple scale pattern with metronome

Metronome method

Start at a tempo you can play perfectly for 30 seconds.

Increase by 2–5 BPM. If mistakes happen, drop back and rebuild.

Rule: accuracy first, speed second.

Clean technique checklist

  • Relax shoulders and wrists
  • Use minimal finger movement
  • Keep a consistent pick angle
  • Even volume across notes
